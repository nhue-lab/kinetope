KINETOPE

A gallery of web stories.

Each piece is a small web experience built to make you feel one thing — told in scroll, shipped in a few kilobytes.

Painters paint canvases. Directors shoot films. These are my pieces: stories that live in a page, move with your scroll, and end when they have made their point. Every work is self-contained, offline-proof, and yours to replay.

l'intention — in french, with intent

A terminal talks to you. It knows you scrolled, it asks who you are, it grows impatient if you don't answer. Then a rabbit shows up — and was already there. The emotion targeted: the slight chill of being watched by a machine that has a sense of humor.

la mécanique

A museum-hub with two rooms: a Matrix-inspired narrative terminal (typing text, impatient questions, a story that remembers your answer until the end), and a visit counter frozen at 000001 since 2003. Text is the only material — no images, no fonts, ~15 KB per room. Scroll is the metronome: each block snaps into frame, the story advances at your hand's pace.
